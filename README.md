# Vanilla UI+

RimWorld UI improvements that stay close to the vanilla look. Most features can be turned off on their own.

- **HUD:** alerts, letters, date, weather and speed controls drawn as tidy equal-width bars, raid letters marked as preparing or immediate, a precise clock, a day counter, colony wealth, a temperature tint, World Domination's threat as a bar, five speed buttons with adjustable tick rates, and default on/off states for the play buttons in new games.
- **Alerts:** right-click to snooze an alert for a few days, plus new alerts for hostiles present, bleeding out, traders available and low batteries.
- **Pawn inspect pane:** armor, movement, work speed and damage per second cards, skills, needs and the current activity at a glance, for your own pawns and, unless turned off, enemies and visitors, with a self-tend toggle. It supports Combat Extended. Wild animals show their butchering yields and revenge chance.
- **Gear tab:** mass carried, comfortable temperature, armor and damage at the top, a row of apparel slots that shows what's missing and opens a menu of apparel to fill it, health bars and warnings on items, and buttons to move gear to and from the inventory.
- **Auto Hunt:** a settings button beside its Wildlife toggle, and its toggle and Auto Tame's settings button kept visible on Numbers' wild animals table.
- **Main menu bar:** reorder, hide or sort tabs into your own named dropdown menus, change their icons, and choose icon, text or both.
- **Pawn tables:** shift a whole schedule an hour earlier or later, see a line at the current time, group the Animals tab by species under collapsible headers that set training, master, medical care, area and more for the whole species, group the Wildlife tab the same way with species-wide hunt and tame checkboxes, and save a pawn's schedule, work priorities or assignments as the default for new colonists, or load the default onto one pawn or the whole tab, with a separate default for slaves.
- **New games:** scenarios sorted by tech level, and saved defaults for the storyteller, world settings and quest reward preferences.
- **Title screen:** a Continue button, and the Tutorial button can be hidden.
- **Search:** map and world search results grouped by kind, a world search across every land tile, and an advanced world search that combines conditions such as biome, temperature, growing period and stone.
- **Resources:** drag to reorder or regroup the resource readout, hide rows, show zero counts and count items across the whole map.
- **Placing zones and buildings:** the fertility overlay turns on while placing a growing zone, and the terrain affordance overlay while placing a building that needs suitable ground.
- **Growing zones:** the pane shows the crop, growth, plants ready to harvest, growing period, fertility and how many people a full harvest feeds as cards, with any blight below.
- **Buildings, items and storage:** cards in the pane show a workbench's bills, worker and work speed; a research bench's project, progress, researcher and speed; a facility's bonuses and links; a building's power, battery charge, grid surplus, fuel, and a heater's or cooler's target temperature and power use; an item's quality or stack, value, hit points, a weapon's damage per second or apparel's armor, nutrition, freshness and deterioration; a stockpile's or shelf's space, contents, value, priority and filter; a plant's growth, growth rate and harvest; and for other buildings, such as walls and ancient junk, their value, beauty, cover and deconstruction or mining yield.
- **Recreation buildings:** selecting one lists its recreation type in the pane.
