# Vanilla UI+

RimWorld UI improvements that stay close to the vanilla look. Every part can be turned off on its own.

- **HUD:** alerts, letters, date, weather and speed controls drawn as tidy equal-width bars, with a precise clock, a day counter, colony wealth, a temperature tint and five speed buttons with adjustable tick rates.
- **Alerts:** right-click to snooze an alert for a few days, plus new alerts for hostiles present, bleeding out, traders available and low batteries.
- **Pawn inspect pane:** armor, comfort range, movement and work speed, damage per second, skills, needs and the current activity at a glance, with a self-tend toggle. It supports Combat Extended. Wild animals show their butchering yields and revenge chance.
- **Main menu bar:** reorder, hide or move tabs into a More menu, change their icons, and choose icon, text or both.
- **Pawn tables:** shift a whole schedule an hour earlier or later, see a line at the current time, and pin a pawn's schedule, work priorities or assignments as the default for new colonists.
- **New games:** scenarios sorted by tech level, and saved defaults for the storyteller, world settings and quest reward preferences.
- **Title screen:** a Continue button, and the Tutorial button can be hidden.
- **Search:** map and world search results grouped by kind, a world search across every land tile, and an advanced world search that combines conditions such as biome, temperature, growing period and stone.
- **Resources:** drag to reorder or regroup the resource readout, hide rows, show zero counts and count items across the whole map.
- **Growing zones:** the fertility overlay turns on while placing a growing zone.
