I like what the recent UI mods are doing, but they are complicated. I am a simple man and I like vanilla's interface. So this is my take on it: vanilla style UI with a few quality of life features. Not a complete overhaul, not separated into modules. One mod, one interface. Simple.

All controlled by the settings play button (look for the cog). A few related mods can have their own settings opened from there too, when they are installed.

[h1]HUD (bottom right)[/h1]
[list]
[*]Alerts, letters, date, weather, speed controls and play setting icons drawn as equal width bars.
[*]Labels stay on one line with an ellipsis. Wrapping is optional.
[*]Optional temperature tint, always-on outdoor temperature, day/night clock tint, colony day counter, and a colony wealth line with an items/buildings/pawns breakdown on hover.
[*]Five speed buttons, including ultrafast without development mode. Tick rate per speed is configurable.
[*]Configurable event speed: choose how fast the game runs when a raid forces a slowdown.
[*]Reverse alert and letter order.
[*]Hide individual play setting buttons.
[*]Hide the speed buttons while keeping their hotkeys.
[*]Adjustable bar background opacity.
[*]Can be turned off to restore vanilla drawing without disabling the rest of the mod.
[*]Filter play buttons.
[*]Combat Extended's wind readout is drawn as one of the bars, shortened to fit, instead of overhanging the column.
[/list]

[h1]Notifications[/h1]
[list]
[*][b]Hostiles present.[/b] Pinned above letters rather than sitting in the alert queue.
[*][b]Bleeding out.[/b] A colonist or prisoner will die of blood loss within about 18 hours.
[*][b]Trader available.[/b] A trade caravan or orbital trade ship is still there to trade with. Vanilla only sends a letter on arrival.
[*][b]Batteries low.[/b] A power grid is draining its batteries and is below either a charge percentage or a time remaining threshold. Both are configurable, and grids that are charging are ignored.
[*]Right click any alert to snooze that type of alert for a set number of in-game days. Default 3. Snoozes are saved with the world and can be cleared from the options.
[*]Hides vanilla's "need a research bench" warning until research has actually unlocked one. Only matters alongside mods that put the bench behind research.
[*]Every notification and snoozing itself can be turned off individually, and they work whether or not the custom HUD is enabled.
[/list]

[h1]Main menu buttons[/h1]
[list]
[*]Drag to reorder tabs.
[*]Move tabs into a More menu, or hide them. Hidden tabs keep their hotkeys.
[*]Change any button's icon, use the icon its own mod provides, or use none. 80 icons included.
[*]Icon only, text and icon, or text only, set per button.
[*]Adds a play settings button that opens the mod options.
[/list]

[h1]Colonist bar[/h1]
[list]
[*]Moves down clear of the development mode toolbar, which otherwise covers the top row of portraits. Only while development mode is on, and the distance is adjustable.
[/list]
