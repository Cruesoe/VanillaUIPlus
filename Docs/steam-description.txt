[b]Small RimWorld UI improvements that stay close to the vanilla look.[/b]

I like vanilla's interface, so this is my take on it: one configurable mod with vanilla-style UI and a set of quality-of-life features.

[h2]HUD[/h2]
[list]
[*]Keeps alerts, letters, date, weather and speed controls in tidy equal-width bars, with adjustable width, opacity, wrapping, order and letter limit.
[*]Shows threat points on raid letters and marks whether raiders are preparing or attacking immediately.
[*]Adds an optional precise clock, colony day counter, colony wealth line and outdoor temperature with temperature and day/night tints.
[*]Offers five configurable speeds, including ultrafast without development mode, and lets you choose what happens when an event slows the game.
[*]Hides any speed or play-setting buttons you don't use.
[*]Lets you turn off the custom HUD and keep the rest of the mod.
[/list]

[h2]Alerts[/h2]
[list]
[*]Adds alerts for hostiles on the map, colonists or prisoners bleeding out, traders still available, and low or draining batteries.
[*]Lets you right-click any alert to snooze it for a set number of days.
[*]Hides the research-bench warning until a bench is unlocked.
[/list]

[h2]Inspect pane[/h2]
[list]
[*]Shows a taller two-column pane for colonists, slaves, prisoners and, unless turned off, everyone else, with vanilla's health, mood, schedule and area bars kept on top.
[*]Shows armor, move speed, work speed and damage per second as cards with detailed tooltips.
[*]Lists skills with passions and aptitudes, plus the current activity, weapon, inspiration and an apparel policy box.
[*]Shows bars for diseases against immunity, life threats, bleeding, pain, untended conditions and low needs when they matter, with color-coded health and mood.
[*]Puts xenotype, gender and age in the header, with buttons to rename the pawn, toggle self-tend and show every need.
[*]Shows cards for animals and other creatures: armor, yields, revenge chance, speed and damage.
[/list]

[h2]Main bar and title screen[/h2]
[list]
[*]Lets you reorder, rename and hide bottom tabs or group them into your own dropdown menus, keeping their hotkeys.
[*]Shows buttons as icons, text or both, with an icon collection to choose from.
[*]Hides the Tutorial button and adds a Continue button that loads your latest save.
[/list]

[h2]Resources, overlays and filters[/h2]
[list]
[*]Lets you reorder, recategorize and hide resources, list ones you have none of, and count everything on the map instead of only what is stored.
[*]Makes item-filter windows resizable, starts their categories collapsed and focuses the search box.
[*]Turns on the fertility overlay while drawing growing zones and the terrain affordance overlay while placing buildings, then restores your previous overlay.
[*]Shows a selected recreation building's recreation type.
[/list]

[h2]Map and world search[/h2]
[list]
[*]Groups search results by kind with counts; hovering a map group points at every item in it.
[*]Searches every land tile by biome, terrain, roads, rivers and stone, and tints the results on the planet, optionally within a destination's range ring.
[*]Builds advanced world searches from dragged conditions such as temperature, growing period and settlement distance, which you can save and load.
[/list]

[h2]Colonists and pawn tables[/h2]
[list]
[*]Groups the Animals tab by species, with controls that set training, master, medical care and area for a whole species.
[*]Groups the Wildlife tab by species, showing each species' leather and revenge chances, with checkboxes that mark a whole species for hunting or taming.
[*]Lets you pin a pawn's schedule, work priorities or Assign settings as the default for future colonists.
[*]Shifts schedules earlier or later and draws the current time across them.
[*]Lets you shift-click an allowed area to assign it to everyone.
[*]Shows leather types on the Wildlife tab and moves the colonist bar clear of the development-mode toolbar.
[/list]

[h2]New games[/h2]
[list]
[*]Remembers your storyteller, difficulty, commitment mode, world generation (with a random seed), goodwill and honor reward choices.
[*]Sorts and colors scenarios by technology level.
[/list]

[h2]Shortcuts[/h2]
Three optional, rebindable shortcuts: Home allows every forbidden item, Backslash toggles the temperature overlay and Numpad Minus toggles development mode.

[h2]Settings[/h2]
Open the settings from the cog beside the play controls. Most features can be switched off, and each page has its own reset button.

[h2]Compatibility[/h2]
Safe to add to an existing save.
[list]
[*]Combat Extended: armor cards show sharp, blunt and heat values, ranged damage counts the loaded ammo, and the pane shows the loadout.
[*]World Domination: its threat alert becomes a bar under the date, with the current stage.
[*]Auto Hunt gets a settings button on the Wildlife tab, and Vanilla Skills Expanded expertise shows in skill tooltips.
[*]The FPS and TPS counters from Dubs Performance Analyzer and FPS And TPS are drawn as bars above the speed controls.
[*]The inspect pane steps aside for RimHUD and Better Inspect Pane, the schedule time line for Chronos Pointer, and overlapping shortcuts for Allow Tool and Keyz' Allow Utilities.
[*]Not compatible with RADMIKO Multiplayer.
[/list]

Mods covering the same features are marked incompatible: Dev Mode Hotkey, Remove Tutorial Button, Continue Button, Precise Time, Smart Speed, UI Not Included, We Had a Trader?, Power Alerts, Resize Filter Tab, 1trickPwnyta's Defaults, Shift Schedule, Custom Resource Readout, Improved Map Search and Auto Hunt Tweaks.
