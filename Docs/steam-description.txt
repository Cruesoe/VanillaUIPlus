[b]Small RimWorld UI improvements that stay close to the vanilla look.[/b]

I like what the recent UI mods are doing, but they are complicated. I am a simple man and I like vanilla's interface. This is my take on it: one configurable mod with vanilla-style UI and a few quality of life features.

[h2]HUD[/h2]
[list]
[*]Keeps alerts, letters, date, weather and controls in equal-width bars.
[*]Lets you adjust width, opacity, text wrapping and notification order.
[*]Marks raid letters "(Preparing)" or "(Immediate)" so you know whether raiders attack straight away.
[*]Adds an optional minute-by-minute clock, colony day counter and colony wealth display.
[*]Shows outdoor temperature with optional temperature and day/night coloring.
[*]Offers five configurable game speeds, including optional ultrafast without development mode.
[*]Lets you choose what happens when an event forces the game to slow down.
[*]Hides unwanted speed and play-setting buttons.
[*]Lets you turn off the custom HUD without disabling the rest of the mod.
[/list]

[h2]Notifications[/h2]
[list]
[*]Adds alerts for hostiles present, colonists or prisoners bleeding out, traders still available, and low or rapidly draining batteries.
[*]Hides the research-bench warning until a bench is unlocked.
[*]Lets you right-click alerts to snooze them for a configurable number of days, and turn each added alert on or off.
[*]Keeps notifications working when the custom HUD is disabled.
[/list]

[h2]Inspect pane[/h2]
[list]
[*]Shows a taller two-column pane for colonists, slaves and prisoners (and optionally enemies, visitors and traders), keeping the vanilla health, mood, schedule and area bars on top.
[*]Shows armor, move speed, work speed and damage per second as cards, each with a detailed tooltip.
[*]Displays skills with passions and an aptitude mark, plus the current activity and weapon. Skill tooltips list the stats each skill affects, and any Vanilla Skills Expanded expertise.
[*]Adds an apparel policy box beside the allowed area box, and shows the current inspiration.
[*]Shows diseases against immunity, life threats, untended conditions, pain, bleeding and low needs as bars when they matter; a header button shows a bar for every need.
[*]Color-codes health and mood bars, matching Color Coded Mood Bar's colors.
[*]Shows xenotype, gender and age in the header, with buttons to rename the pawn (name and title) and toggle self-tend.
[*]Shows wild animals' butchering and shearing yields and their revenge chance on harm.
[*]Steps aside automatically when RimHUD or Better Inspect Pane is active.
[/list]

[h2]Main bar and title screen[/h2]
[list]
[*]Reorders and renames bottom tabs and sorts them into your own named dropdown menus, or hides them, while keeping their hotkeys.
[*]Offers icon-only, text-only or combined buttons.
[*]Lets you change button icons using the included icon collection.
[*]Hides the Tutorial button.
[*]Adds a Continue button that loads your most recently updated save.
[/list]

[h2]Resources and filters[/h2]
[list]
[*]Lets you reorder resources or move them between categories.
[*]Hides unwanted resources or shows resources with a zero count.
[*]Counts everything on the map instead of only stored items.
[*]Starts item-filter categories collapsed.
[*]Focuses the storage search box automatically.
[*]Lets you resize item-filter windows.
[*]Automatically shows fertility while drawing growing zones, then restores your previous overlay choice.
[/list]

[h2]Map and world search[/h2]
[list]
[*]Groups search results by kind with counts; hovering a map group points at every item in it.
[*]Searches every land tile in the world and can match biomes, terrain, roads, rivers and stone types.
[*]Tints world results on the planet and optionally limits them to a destination's range ring.
[*]Lets you build advanced world searches by dragging conditions such as biome, temperature, growing period, stone and settlement distance into all of, any of and none of groups.
[*]Lets you save and load advanced searches.
[/list]

[h2]Colonists and pawn tables[/h2]
[list]
[*]Moves the colonist bar clear of the development-mode toolbar.
[*]Shows leather types in the Wildlife tab.
[*]Lets you shift-click to assign an allowed area to everyone.
[*]Shifts individual or colony-wide schedules earlier or later.
[*]Lets you pin a pawn's schedule, work priorities or Assign settings as the default for future colonists.
[*]Shows the current time as a line across pawn schedules.
[*]Groups the Animals tab by species under collapsible headers with a count for each, and controls on each header that set training, master, medical care, allowed area and the other checkboxes for the whole species.
[/list]

[h2]New-game defaults[/h2]
[list]
[*]Saves storyteller, difficulty and commitment-mode choices.
[*]Saves world-generation settings while keeping the seed random.
[*]Saves default goodwill and honor quest-reward preferences.
[*]Sorts and colors scenarios by technology level.
[/list]

[h2]Quick controls[/h2]
Three optional shortcuts, each rebindable:
[list]
[*]Home allows every forbidden item.
[*]Backslash toggles the vanilla temperature overlay.
[*]Numpad Minus toggles development mode.
[/list]

[h2]Settings[/h2]
Open the settings from the cog beside the play controls. Most features can be switched off, and each page has its own reset button.
[list]
[*][b]HUD[/b]: bars, alerts, date, temperature and game speeds.
[*][b]Notifications[/b]: added alerts and snoozing.
[*][b]Interface[/b]: main bar, title screen, resources, inspect pane, search and pawn tables.
[*][b]Defaults[/b]: pinned pawn, new-game and quest-reward defaults.
[*][b]Controls[/b]: shortcuts and mod integrations.
[/list]

[h2]Compatibility[/h2]
Safe to add to an existing save.

Fully compatible with Combat Extended: the armor cards show its sharp (mm RHA) and blunt (MPa) numbers and heat from the Gear tab, and ranged damage counts the loaded ammo and its pellets. The inspect pane steps aside for RimHUD and Better Inspect Pane. The current-time line steps aside for Chronos Pointer. The FPS and TPS counters from Dubs Performance Analyzer and FPS And TPS are drawn as bars above the speed controls. Overlapping shortcuts automatically disable themselves when Allow Tool or Keyz' Allow Utilities provides the same feature.

Mods that cover the same features are marked incompatible so they do not clash: Dev Mode Hotkey, Remove Tutorial Button, Continue Button, Precise Time, Smart Speed, UI Not Included, We Had a Trader?, Power Alerts, Resize Filter Tab, 1trickPwnyta's Defaults, Shift Schedule, Custom Resource Readout and Improved Map Search.
