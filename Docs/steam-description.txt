I like what the recent UI mods are doing, but they are complicated. I am a simple man and I like vanilla's interface. So this is my take on it: vanilla style UI with a few quality of life features. Not a complete overhaul, not separated into modules. One mod, one interface. Simple.

Everything is configurable from the settings cog beside the play controls.

[h1]HUD[/h1]
[list]
[*]Equal-width bars for alerts, letters, date, weather and controls.
[*]Adjustable width, opacity, text wrapping and notification order.
[*]Optional minute-by-minute clock, colony day counter and colony wealth display.
[*]Outdoor temperature with optional temperature and day/night colouring.
[*]Five configurable game speeds, including optional ultrafast without development mode.
[*]Choose what happens when an event forces the game to slow down.
[*]Hide unwanted speed and play-setting buttons.
[*]Turn off the custom HUD without disabling the rest of the mod.
[/list]

[h1]Notifications[/h1]
[list]
[*]Alerts for hostiles present, colonists or prisoners bleeding out, traders still available, and low or rapidly draining batteries.
[*]Hide the research-bench warning until a bench is unlocked.
[*]Right-click alerts to snooze them for a configurable number of days.
[*]Enable or disable each added alert individually.
[*]Notifications continue to work when the custom HUD is disabled.
[/list]

[h1]Inspect pane[/h1]
[list]
[*]A taller two-column pane for colonists, slaves and prisoners, keeping the vanilla health, mood, schedule and area bars on top.
[*]Armor, a comfort thermometer, move and work speed, and damage with hit chance, each with a thin gauge and a detailed tooltip.
[*]Skills with passions and aptitude-tinted bars, plus the current activity and weapon.
[*]Bleeding time and low needs appear only when they matter.
[*]Colour-coded health and mood bars, matching Color Coded Mood Bar's colours.
[*]Xenotype, gender and age in the header, with buttons to rename the pawn (name and title) and toggle self-tend.
[*]Wild animals show their butchering and shearing yields and their revenge chance on harm.
[*]Steps aside automatically when RimHUD or Better Inspect Pane is active.
[/list]

[h1]Main bar and title screen[/h1]
[list]
[*]Reorder bottom tabs from the mod settings.
[*]Move tabs into a More menu or hide them while keeping their hotkeys.
[*]Choose icon-only, text-only or combined buttons.
[*]Change button icons using the included icon collection.
[*]Hide the Tutorial button.
[*]Add a Continue button that loads your most recently updated save.
[/list]

[h1]Resources and filters[/h1]
[list]
[*]Reorder resources or move them between categories.
[*]Hide unwanted resources or show resources with a zero count.
[*]Count everything on the map instead of only stored items.
[*]Start item-filter categories collapsed.
[*]Focus the storage search box automatically.
[*]Resize item-filter windows.
[/list]

[h1]Map and world search[/h1]
[list]
[*]Search results grouped by kind with counts; hover a map group to point at every item in it.
[*]World search covers every land tile and can match biomes, terrain, roads, rivers and stone types.
[*]World results tinted on the planet, and optionally limited to a destination's range ring.
[*]Advanced world search: drag conditions like biome, temperature, growing period, stone and settlement distance into all of, any of and none of groups.
[*]Save and load advanced searches.
[*]Based on the ideas of kathanon's Improved Map Search, rewritten for Vanilla UI+.
[/list]

[h1]Colonists and pawn tables[/h1]
[list]
[*]Move the colonist bar clear of the development-mode toolbar.
[*]Show leather types in the Wildlife tab.
[*]Shift-click to assign an allowed area to everyone.
[*]Shift individual or colony-wide schedules earlier or later.
[*]Pin a pawn's schedule, work priorities or Assign settings as the default for future colonists.
[/list]

[h1]New-game defaults[/h1]
[list]
[*]Save storyteller, difficulty and commitment-mode choices.
[*]Save world-generation settings while keeping the seed random.
[*]Save default goodwill and honor quest-reward preferences.
[*]Sort and colour scenarios by technology level.
[/list]

[h1]Quick controls[/h1]
Three optional, rebindable shortcuts are included:

[list]
[*]Home — allow every forbidden item.
[*]Backslash — toggle the vanilla temperature overlay.
[*]Numpad Minus — toggle development mode.
[/list]

Overlapping shortcuts automatically disable themselves when Allow Tool, Keyz' Allow Utilities or Heat Map provides the same feature.

[h1]Everything is optional[/h1]
Settings are organized into HUD, Notifications, Interface, Defaults and Controls. Every major feature can be switched off, and each page has its own reset button.
