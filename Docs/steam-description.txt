[b]Small RimWorld UI improvements that stay close to the vanilla look.[/b]

I like vanilla's interface, so this is my take on it. Most features can be switched off on their own. For more detail, see the pinned feature guide in the [url=https://steamcommunity.com/workshop/filedetails/discussions/3793535492]Discussions[/url] tab.

[h2]Features[/h2]
[list]
[*][b]HUD:[/b] Alerts, letters, date, weather and speed controls sit in tidy equal-width bars, with a precise clock, day counter, colony wealth, outdoor temperature and five adjustable game speeds.
[*][b]Raid letters:[/b] Each raid shows its threat points and whether the raiders are preparing or attacking now.
[*][b]Alerts:[/b] New alerts warn of hostiles, bleeding out, traders still available and low batteries, and any alert can be snoozed with a right-click.
[*][b]Inspect pane:[/b] A taller two-column pane shows armor, movement, work speed and damage as cards, with skills, needs and the current activity at a glance.
[*][b]Gear tab:[/b] Mass, comfortable temperature, armor and damage sit at the top, above a row of apparel slots that marks what's missing and fills it from a menu.
[*][b]Main bar:[/b] Bottom tabs can be reordered, renamed, hidden or grouped into your own dropdown menus.
[*][b]Pawn tables:[/b] The Animals and Wildlife tabs group by species, schedules shift an hour at a time, and schedules, work priorities and assignments can be saved as defaults for new colonists.
[*][b]Resources:[/b] The resource readout can be reordered, regrouped and filtered, and can count everything on the map.
[*][b]Search:[/b] Map and world search results are grouped by kind, and an advanced world search finds tiles by biome, temperature, growing period, stone and more.
[*][b]New games:[/b] Your storyteller, difficulty and world settings are remembered, and scenarios are sorted by tech level.
[*][b]Small touches:[/b] The title screen gets a Continue button, overlays turn on while you place zones and buildings, item filters can be resized, and there are three optional shortcuts.
[/list]

[h2]Settings[/h2]
Open the settings from the cog beside the play controls. Each page has its own reset button.

[h2]Compatibility[/h2]
Safe to add to an existing save.
[list]
[*]Supports Combat Extended's armor, ammo and loadouts, World Domination's threat, Auto Hunt, Vanilla Skills Expanded, Dubs Performance Analyzer and FPS And TPS.
[*]Steps aside for RimHUD, Better Inspect Pane, Nice Inventory Tab, RPG Style Inventory, UI Rethemed, Chronos Pointer, Allow Tool and Keyz' Allow Utilities.
[*]Mods that do the same jobs are flagged as incompatible in the mod list.
[*]Not compatible with RADMIKO Multiplayer.
[/list]
